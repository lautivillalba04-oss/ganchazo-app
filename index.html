Ganchazo

Ganchazo is a personal desktop tool that turns long videos (vlogs, podcasts, streams) into short vertical clips with a title and subtitles.

It runs only on the creator's own computer. When the creator chooses, a finished clip can be sent to their own TikTok inbox as a draft, to their own YouTube channel, or to their own Instagram account. Nothing is posted without the creator pressing the button, and drafts are reviewed and published by the creator inside the TikTok app.
