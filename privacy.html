Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Ganchazo runs entirely on the owner's computer. There is no Ganchazo server that collects user data.

What we access

  • TikTok: your basic profile (display name) to show which account is connected, and permission to upload videos to your inbox as drafts (scopes user.info.basic and video.upload).
  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to your channel and read your channel name.
  • Instagram: permission to publish reels you choose and read their statistics.

How it is stored and used

  • Access tokens are stored only on your computer, in a file readable only by your user. They are never sent anywhere except to the platform that issued them.
  • Videos are sent only to the platform you choose, only when you press upload.
  • We do not sell, share or analyze your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Deleting your data

Use "Disconnect" in Ganchazo to delete the stored tokens, or revoke access from your TikTok, Google or Instagram account settings at any time.
